Choices made inside the design:

- Options sit under the third label after a blank line; a label's final period is dropped.
- `answer` refuses without `--area`, `--amends` or `--no-entry`.
- A render keeps a block only when its labels, options, marks, answer line and numbers are unchanged; the contract goes to the renderer as a temp file beside the questions file, with `RESPEAK_DEPTH=1` and a 600 s wall.
- L1 takes a clock time between the date and the `;` or `,`, since the cross-repo ledger's entries 2, 10 and 11 carry one (`2026-09-25 ~16:15;`).
- L3 lives in `ledger.lint`, which `render` and `doctor` call; `load` stays tolerant and reads the ledger alone.
- The one-to-one check is two problems, a question without its Proposed line and a Proposed line without its question, only when the questions file exists.
- The sample fixture gains `OPEN-QUESTIONS.md` with question 8, so the tests that lint after `new` pair the sample's Proposed entry 8.
- Numbers count parsed entries only: numbers below the marker reserve none, since that text is not entries.
- A legacy block for `find` is a paragraph, a list item or a heading; `-` stands for no heading. `show --area` on a legacy heading prints it and its text to the next heading of its level or above; a parsed area wins a tie.
